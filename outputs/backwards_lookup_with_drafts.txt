SIMULATION AND RECORDS ONLY: comparative, not predictive. Shares are read from each record's free tip curve; nothing is simulated.

The turn needs each fingertip to move along the azimuth at its contact. At contact 0 that direction is turned into the finger's own frame by the mount rotation.
For each record, the steps of its free tip curve are summed as dy dy^T / |du| over part of its input range; the share is the part of that sum along the needed direction (1 = all of it).
Parts of the range: the first third of the steps, the first half, the whole sweep, and the best run of steps at least a third of the sweep long.
Along normal and other tangent: whole-sweep shares along the contact normal and the remaining tangent; with the whole-sweep share they add to one.
Ranked by the first-third share, highest first.
Draft records (flag source:draft_from_published_figures) are digitised from published figures, not reviewed and not in the manifest. Flag frame:shared_three_chamber_frame_not_canonical: the record is not in the canonical frame, so its share reflects the frame, not the actuator. Flag stroke:read_under_preload_tension_not_free: the tip curve was read with the actuator under a steady load.
